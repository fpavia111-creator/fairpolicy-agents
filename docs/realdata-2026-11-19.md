# Real-data tests (2026-11-19)

Made by `node scripts/realdata.mjs --download` (`npm run realdata`). Public, lawful data in its published format, downloaded 2026-11-19 to `_realdata/` (git-ignored, not committed). Each test compares the agent with an independent count written in the script. No customer or private data was used; no names were downloaded.

- **tdi_licenses**: The first 20,000 General Lines Agent licences by licence number. CE hours are not published, so CE itself is not tested here.
- **tdi_companies**: A loose match (one name is the other with words left off the end) is used only when no exact key matches and it points at one entry. Loose pairs (211): "Access Insurance Company" ~ "Access Home Insurance Company"; "Aetna Health Insurance Company" ~ "Aetna Health Inc."; "Aetna Health Insurance Company" ~ "AETNA HEALTH AND LIFE INSURANCE COMPANY"; "Allied World Insurance Company" ~ "Allied World National Assurance Company"; "Allied World Insurance Company" ~ "Allied World Specialty Insurance Company".
- **nyc_fhv**: A VIN that fails the check digit is a typo in the public file or a vehicle built without a North American check digit; the agent asks a person to confirm it, it does not say which.
- **nfip_claims**: Counties stand in for accounts because FEMA removes the insured; there is no premium file, so no loss ratio is tested.

Nothing public fits the other agents: commission statements, agency-bill invoices, trust bank statements, producer pay, certificates, endorsements, premium finance, audits, renewals and download logs are private agency or carrier records. They were tested on fictional data only.
