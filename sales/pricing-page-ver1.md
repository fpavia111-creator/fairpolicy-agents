# Fairpolicy pricing (ver 1)

The software is free (MIT). You pay a fixed price for the setup: we install the agents on your machine, map your exports (policy and expiration lists from Applied Epic, AMS360, Sagitta, HawkSoft, EZLynx or QQCatalyst, carrier commission statements, receivables, the trust account bank export, download logs, certificate logs, licence lists), write your settings with you, and run each agent once on your own files with your team.

## Support after setup

- $250 a month per package.
- $750 a month for the whole agency.
- $2,000 a month for a partner rollout.

Support covers new export layouts when a vendor or carrier changes its columns, setting changes (commission schedules, split percents, bonus grids, state continuing-education hours your agency confirms), and a monthly review call. It can be stopped any month; the agents keep working without it.

## What is and is not included

Included: installation on your machine, column mapping for your exports, settings files written with you, one training session, the first run of every agent in the package on your files, and a written list of what each agent read and what it found.

Not included: binding, cancelling, endorsing or placing coverage; advising clients; paying carriers or producers; moving trust-account money; issuing certificates; sending anything to a carrier or client; legal, tax, accounting, licensing or compliance opinions; and any connection to a carrier portal or your agency system's live database. The agents read files you export and write worklists and drafts for a named person to act on.

## Decisions written down

- Prices sit in the $1,000 to $15,000 range set for every Fair Agents family and in line with Fairwire and Fairtable: $1,500 to $2,500 for a group of agents by the number of exports to map, $9,500 for the whole family, $15,000 for a partner rollout.
- Agency bill and trust account, renewals and the service desk are $2,500 because each maps four to six different exports; commission check is $2,000 because carrier statements differ by carrier.
- Book summary is $1,000: one agent, one export, often bought before a perpetuation or sale conversation.
- Partner rollout is aimed at agency networks, clusters and aggregators that serve many member agencies, the insurance version of an MSP.
